How it works

From link to dub in five steps

Paste a YouTube URL (or upload a file) and DubAI handles the rest — powered by ElevenLabs.

1

Ingest

Pull audio straight from a YouTube link or your uploaded file.

2

Transcribe

Speech is transcribed with per-speaker timing.

3

Translate

Dialogue is translated into your target language.

4

Clone voice

Each original voice is cloned and speaks the translation.

5

Deliver

Download a finished, timed, dubbed audio track.
